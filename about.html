About QBank

A focused academic resource designed to make exam preparation simpler.

Student-first by design

One organized place for the questions that matter.

UniQBank is a student-focused platform for organizing and accessing past examination questions. It replaces scattered files and uncertain links with a structured academic library students can understand immediately.

The platform groups resources by level, college, course, and session so every student can move from search to study without friction.

Our mission

“To make academic preparation more accessible by giving every student a clear, dependable path to past examination resources.”
